01The idea

LocationAhmedabad, Gujarat
Year2025
TypePrivate residence
ScopeFull service

A calm home shaped by sunlight, raw tactility and daily ritual.

The house began with a desire for togetherness without constant exposure. Curved thresholds soften the plan, garden courts create moments of pause and a measured palette allows light to become the primary decoration.

Earthy plaster, honed travertine and teak establish a restrained material language. Built-in furniture continues the architecture at a smaller scale, while objects collected over time give the rooms their particular warmth.

02Material language

Quiet, but never blank.

01

02

03
