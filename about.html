about us

Radhe is a residential and workplace architecture practice founded in 2013 by Radhe Suthar. We work on new homes, extensions, refurbishments and interiors across Ahmedabad, Gujarat and western India.

Our first work on any building is understanding what is already possible within it. The strategy follows from that reading, not from a preferred outcome. What we add is judged on what it takes to produce and how long it lasts. We are interested in how buildings are made, how materials age, and how early decisions determine what a house is like to live in twenty years on.

Drawing is central to how we work. Plans, sections and axonometrics run through every stage, used to test ideas and to make them legible — so you can see what is being proposed rather than take it on trust.

Alongside houses, our experience covers hospitality, workplace and heritage-sensitive work, and we take on retail and community projects where the fit is right.
