Designing from the inside out.

Ahmedabad · Gujarat · India

We are a close-knit interior architecture practice creating spaces with emotional clarity, material honesty and a distinctly human rhythm.

01Our story

A home should hold your story—not simply follow a style.

Founded in Ahmedabad, Radhe brings architecture, interiors, furniture and execution under one roof. Our process begins by listening closely, then translating daily rituals into spatial experiences that feel both unexpected and completely natural.

We work with a limited number of clients at a time. That closeness lets the same creative intent travel from the earliest sketch through site decisions, custom joinery and the last object placed.

02What guides us

Principles over trends.

Our work changes from place to place. The way we think does not.

01 · Listen first

Life leads the plan.

We study how people move, gather, rest and change before deciding how a space should look.

02 · Make honestly

Materials tell the truth.

Stone, timber, metal and textiles are selected for how they age, feel and belong to their context.

03 · Resolve deeply

Beauty lives in joins.

Proportion, shadow, touch and the meeting of materials receive the same care as the larger gesture.
