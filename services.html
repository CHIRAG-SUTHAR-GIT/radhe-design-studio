services

One studio, from the first sketch to the last fixing.

We can take a project end to end, or step in at the stage where you need us. Either way the same team carries the drawings through.

We begin by listening. Understanding your brief, your site and what the development control rules actually allow lets us shape realistic, ambitious proposals that sit inside your budget.

  • Site appraisal and measured survey
  • Development potential and massing studies
  • Budget and programme framing
  • Statutory and approvals strategy

Plans, sections and material palettes developed together and tested against Indian light and climate, then carried through approvals with the drawings and arguments that get them consented.

  • Concept and spatial planning
  • Daylight, shade and ventilation studies
  • Material and finish palettes
  • Approval drawings and submissions

We produce comprehensive technical packages for approval and contractor pricing. Our focus on materiality and construction logic keeps designs buildable, durable and true to the original idea.

  • Working drawings and specifications
  • Joinery and bespoke furniture details
  • Services and consultant coordination
  • Tender packages and cost review

We work alongside contractors through the build — answering queries, reviewing work on site and holding quality and design intent all the way to handover.

  • Contractor selection and tendering
  • Site supervision and quality control
  • Snagging, styling and handover
  • Post-completion review
