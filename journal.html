01From the notebook

Ideas worth keeping.

01

Planning a home that grows with you

Start with the parts of life most likely to change: children, work, care and gathering. Rooms that borrow light, accept more than one furniture arrangement and include useful thresholds can evolve without losing their identity.

02

Inside our material library

We keep samples for years, not weeks. Seeing how wood darkens, brass develops a patina and stone responds to use is more valuable than judging a finish on the day it arrives.

03

Why good studios make room for pause

Creative focus depends on variation. Alongside worktables and meeting rooms, we plan small places to step away, look outside and let an unfinished idea settle.

04

The enduring warmth of natural stone

Stone is never only a colour. Grain, porosity, edge profile and the way light moves across its finish determine whether it reads as cool, formal, grounded or quietly soft.
