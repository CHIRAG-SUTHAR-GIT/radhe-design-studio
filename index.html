Our process, your advantage

From the first survey to the final walkthrough.

We have become experts at getting interiors built exactly as drawn. We design carefully and detail obsessively. And we care deeply about what we hand over.

  • 01Discovery

    Site survey & brief

    Every project starts on site. We measure what exists, record the light through the day, and lock a brief everyone signs.

  • 02Concept

    Plan, mood & material direction

    Layouts are tested against how you actually live. Two or three plans, one mood direction, and samples you can hold.

  • 03Detailing

    Working drawings & specifications

    Joinery sections, lighting plans and finish schedules, drawn so a carpenter can build from them. Nothing is guessed on site.

  • 04Execution

    Procurement, site & quality

    Materials are ordered against the schedule, not memory. We run the site week by week and report in photographs.

  • 05Handover

    Styling, snag close & aftercare

    The snag list is closed before you move in, not after. Rooms are styled and lighting balanced scene by scene. We return after the first monsoon to check joinery and finishes, and put right whatever has moved.
